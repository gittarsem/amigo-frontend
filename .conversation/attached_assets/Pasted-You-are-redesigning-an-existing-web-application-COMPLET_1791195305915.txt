You are redesigning an existing web application.

COMPLETELY REDESIGN THE FRONTEND INTO A FRESH, MINIMAL, AESTHETIC, GEN-Z SOCIAL DISCOVERY PRODUCT CALLED:

AMIGO

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT simply modify the existing dashboard.

Do NOT preserve the current visual layout.

============================================================
01 — PRODUCT
============================================================

AMIGO is a social discovery platform.

People use Amigo to:

• discover things happening around them
• discover events and activities
• find people with similar interests
• find someone to go somewhere with
• make plans together
• join communities
• chat with people
• discover new experiences

Core loop:

DISCOVER
    ↓
FIND PEOPLE
    ↓
CONNECT
    ↓
MAKE A PLAN
    ↓
GO TOGETHER

Amigo is NOT a dating app.

It is:

friendship + activities + events + communities + social discovery.

The experience should feel natural for Gen-Z users.
