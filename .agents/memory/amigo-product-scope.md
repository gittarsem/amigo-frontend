---
name: AMIGO product scope
description: The intended purpose and positioning of the AMIGO app.
---

Keep AMIGO focused on helping people find events, meet friends, join communities, chat, and make plans together. It is not a dating app or a dashboard.

**Why:** This is the user's explicit product goal.

**How to apply:** Use this scope when evaluating future features, visual direction, and copy for AMIGO.
